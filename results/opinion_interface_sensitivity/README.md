# Opinion-interface sensitivity

This frozen 256-setting scrambled-Sobol study varies language sharpness,
Dirichlet projection concentration, quality and conflict multipliers, and
occlusion strength while retaining the complete 31,200-record condition mix.
The common admission rule, scene folds, support `[0.10, 0.35]`, and
fold-specific concentration schedule remain fixed.

Complete PACT has ncsAURC 0.0660--0.0691 (median 0.0669), below nested
Dirichlet composition and registered pooling in all 256 settings. The result
characterizes the stated opinion-construction design region; it is not a
raw-sensor or open-world robustness claim.

`PROTOCOL_LOCK.json`, `baseline_gate.json`, `gate.json`, and
`setting_results.csv` are the claim-bearing records. `run.py` is retained as an
archival generation script and depends on the larger experiment workspace.
